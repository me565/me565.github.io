About

The Moth House is a summer mystery for children: three stories, one summer, and a house that appears at dusk to people who are looking.

This is a work of fiction. Its characters, schools and events are imaginary. Any resemblance to real people or places is a coincidence.

Story, puzzles and design by the studio. Pictures and music made with the help of AI tools and drawn into the story by hand. Typefaces: Caprasimo and Atkinson Hyperlegible.
