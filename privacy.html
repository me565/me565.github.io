Privacy policy

The Moth House, by OG Entertainment Studios. In effect from 29 October 2026.

In short: The Moth House collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and nothing to buy. Your progress is saved on your own device and never sent to us.

Who we are

The Moth House is a point-and-click mystery for children, made by OG Entertainment Studios, an independent developer. It can be played at me565.github.io and through the Android app on Google Play, which opens the same website.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for a name, email address, age, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information. It has no sign-in, no chat, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases. The Android app asks for no device permissions.

What is stored on your device

To remember where you are in the story, the game keeps a small save in your browser's local storage on your own device. It holds:

  • each child's progress through the levels, and which one you played last;
  • your settings, such as music and sound volume;
  • whether you have already seen the "How to play" page.

This save stays on your device. We never receive it and cannot see it.

To delete all of it, uninstall the app, clear the app's storage in your device's settings, or clear this website's data in your browser. To reset only the story, use "Start Mary's story from the beginning" in the game's Help tab.

Services the game relies on

Like any website, the game is delivered by other companies, who may receive the technical details every web request carries, such as your device's IP address and browser type. We do not receive or use this information.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the game's typefaces. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play handles downloads and updates of the Android app, and the app opens the game in your device's web browser. Their own privacy policies apply to those services.

Children

The game is made for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. We designed it this way to meet children's privacy laws, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts to collect any information, we will update this page before that change goes live, and change the date at the top.
